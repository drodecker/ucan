# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants should be respectful, technically rigorous, and open to disagreement.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- explaining assumptions;
- distinguishing facts from proposals;
- acknowledging uncertainty;
- criticizing designs without attacking people;
- welcoming newcomers and domain experts alike.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when necessary.

Serious concerns may be reported privately to the repository owner.
