# Governance

## Current status

UCAN does not yet have a formal governance body.

The repository is currently maintained as an open project under its original publisher.

## Governance principles

Any future governance structure should:

- publish decisions and rationale;
- separate technical standards from commercial interests;
- permit public review;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- support multiple implementations;
- avoid permanent founder control;
- include clear amendment and appeal processes;
- preserve the ability to fork under the project's open licenses.

## Possible future structure

A future UCAN organization may include:

- technical steering group;
- operator advisory group;
- security review group;
- documentation maintainers;
- elected or appointed community representatives;
- nonprofit foundation;
- on-chain governance or DAO components.

No particular structure is adopted at this stage.

## Repository authority

Maintainers may merge changes to documentation and draft specifications.

Material protocol changes should include:

1. a written proposal;
2. discussion period;
3. security and interoperability analysis;
4. documented maintainer decision;
5. versioned publication.

## Transition principle

If a company or founder initially funds the project, the project's protocol documents, code, trademarks, and governance should be capable of transitioning to an independent public-interest organization under clear terms.

A fixed transition date should not be adopted until the legal and governance consequences are understood.
